How can we help?

Answers to what people ask most. The same help is in the app under SettingsHelp.

Dynamic Island

My slime disappeared from the Dynamic Island.

iOS ends every Live Activity after about 8 hours. That's a limit of iOS, not of Slimy. Open Slimy and your slime comes right back, or use Summon Slime from Control Center or the Action Button without opening the app.

If it still doesn't appear, check that Live Activities are on for Slimy in the Settings app, and that Always bring it back is on in Slimy's Settings under Dynamic Island.

How do I add Summon Slime to Control Center or the Action Button?

Control Center: open it, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Add a Control and choose Summon Slime.

Action Button, on iPhones that have one: open the Settings app, tap Action Button, swipe to Controls and pick Summon Slime.

The slime in the island doesn't move.

Check that Animation is on in Slimy's Settings under Dynamic Island. With Animation off, the island shows a still picture that changes with your slime's mood.

Why does my slime react late to my iPhone?

While Slimy is open it reacts right away. When it's closed, it notices changes the next time iOS lets it run, for example during background refresh or while charging, so a reaction can come a little later. Make sure Background App Refresh is on for Slimy in the Settings app.

My iPhone has no Dynamic Island.

Slimy works on every iPhone with iOS 18. Without a Dynamic Island, your slime shows on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity and in widgets, and lives in the app as usual.

Your slime

Does my slime have feelings?

Yes. It can feel joy, sadness, anger, fear, love, curiosity, boredom, excitement and sleepiness, some more strongly than others. Meals, games, baths, cuddles, friends and chats change how it feels, and its feelings settle again over time.

Its mood shows under its name on the home screen, in how it moves and in what it says. It never blames you for being away.

Why does my slime keep talking?

While Slimy is open, your slime says what's on its mind every few seconds: how it feels, what it needs, the time of day, the weather or what just happened. It doesn't repeat itself straight away. For a quieter slime, turn off Talks on its own in Settings.

Crates

How do I get crates?

Buy them in the shop with coins: a wooden crate costs 80 coins, a golden crate 300.

Mystery crates can't be bought. One comes with the gift on day 7 of a streak, with every fifth level, with some achievements and, now and then, after a great round in the arcade. Tap a crate a few times to open it.

What's inside a crate?

Coins, food, hats and other things to wear, furniture, new colours for your slime, or even another crate. Rewards are common, rare, epic or legendary. If you get something you already have, it turns into coins instead, and the crate tells you so.

What are the chances?

Tap Drop rates in the shop or next to your crates to see the chances for every kind of crate. Wooden crates never hold a legendary reward; golden and mystery crates can.

What's the legendary reward?

That's a secret. Golden crates hide it, and mystery crates a little more often. Nobody's telling, so you'll know it when you see it.

Do crates cost real money?

No. Crates are earned in the game or bought with coins, and coins can't be bought. Slimy has no in-app purchases.

Care Together

How can two people look after one slime?

Open Care Together in Friends or Settings, and both of you go online. One of you taps Get a Care Code and shares it; the other enters it within 10 minutes and taps Join. A code works once.

From then on both iPhones show the same slime. When either of you feeds it, plays with it, gives it a bath, pets it, puts it to bed, wakes it up or gives it a gift, it happens on the other iPhone too, and you can see who did what and when, like “Ana fed Blob · 5 min ago”.

What happens to my own slime when I join?

It's kept safe on your iPhone while you look after your partner's slime, and it comes back when you stop caring together. If you shared the code, the slime stays yours when you stop.

What is shared, and what stays mine?

Only the slime is shared: its name, colour, level, needs, feelings and what it wears. Your coins, the things you bought, your records, your challenges and your settings stay your own. You can care for one slime with one person at a time.

How do we stop caring together?

Either of you can tap Stop Sharing in Care Together at any time, and the other iPhone stops sharing too. Whoever joined gets their own slime back; the shared slime stays with the person who shared the code.

Beta, install and widgets

Slimy won't open anymore.

With a free Apple ID, sideloaded apps stop opening after 7 days until they're signed again. Connect your iPhone to your computer, open Sideloadly and install the newest Slimy.ipa with the same Apple ID.

Your slime stays as long as you don't delete the app first. Exporting a save now and then, in Settings under Backup, keeps you on the safe side. Step by step: re-signing.

How do I update Slimy?

On iPhone, install the new Slimy.ipa over the current app the same way you installed it, with the same Apple ID, and your slime stays. On Android, download the new Slimy.apk and open it; it installs over the old version. New builds are on the install page.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, as a beta: Slimy.apk, for Android 8 or later. It's the same slime with the same games, crates, friends and chat. Android has no Dynamic Island, so your slime lives in a notification, on the lock screen and in widgets instead.

It isn't on Google Play yet. You download the APK from GitHub and allow your browser to install it once. Friends and caring together work between Android and iPhone. Step by step: install on Android.

My widgets stay empty.

Open Slimy once so the widgets get your slime, then give them a moment. iOS decides how often widgets refresh.

If you installed Slimy-unsigned.ipa, or your sideloading tool removed the App Group, the widgets can't see your slime. Install Slimy.ipa instead to use widgets.

When is Slimy on the App Store?

Slimy: Island Pet is being prepared for App Store review. When it's live, this website will say so and link to it. Until then, the beta is the way to get it.

Friends

How do I add a friend?

Open Friends and go online. Then tap Get My Code and share it, or enter your friend's code and tap Connect. Your friend accepts the request, and your slimes are friends.

My friend's code doesn't work.

A code expires after 10 minutes and works once. If it's older or already used, ask for a new one. Codes look like K7Q2MD: six letters and numbers, without I, L, O, 0 or 1, so they're easy to read aloud.

What can our slimes do together?

Send hugs and gifts, say hello, visit each other, challenge each other to a game, grow your shared garden and compare scores on the leaderboard. Now and then a friend's slime drops by your Dynamic Island. To look after one slime together, use Care Together.

Can strangers find me?

No. There's no search and no public profile. Only people you give a code to can send you a friend request, and you decide whether to accept it. Greetings are a fixed set of phrases, so nobody can send you free text.

How do I report or block someone?

In Friends, tap a friend and choose Report or Block at the bottom of their page, or touch and hold them in the list. For a friend request, tap the three dots next to it.

Blocking ends the friendship right away. Nothing from that device reaches you anymore, and they aren't notified. You can unblock them later under Privacy & blocked in Friends.

A report goes to the Slimy team. A person reviews every report and can reset a name or ban a device. A report doesn't block anyone by itself, so block them too if you don't want to hear from them again.

To report something your slime said in chat, touch and hold the reply and tap Report Reply.

The app says this device can't use online features anymore.

The device was banned after a report, under our Terms of Use. Your slime and everything offline keep working. You can still delete your server data in Settings. If you think this is a mistake, contact us below.

Talking with your slime

What happens to my chat messages?

When Talk with AI is on, your message, the recent chat and a few facts about your slime, like its name, how it feels and its needs, go to Cloudflare Workers AI and Groq, which write the reply. Your name and device ID are never passed on to them, and Slimy's server doesn't keep the messages. You can turn Talk with AI off any time in Settings under Privacy. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does the chat change how my slime feels?

It can. Your slime answers in its current mood, and what you write can cheer it up, calm it down, make it shy or get it excited, just like caring for it does. Its new mood shows on the home screen.

My slime is too sleepy to talk.

Talking uses a limited amount of free AI each day. When it runs out, or the AI services are busy, your slime gets sleepy. Try again later.

My slime said something wrong or strange.

Replies come from an AI model and can be wrong. Your slime is a pet, not an advisor, so don't rely on it for facts or decisions. Touch and hold the reply and tap Report Reply, and we'll look at it.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

Your slime, coins and things live only on your phone. To delete what Slimy's server stores about you (your online profile, your friends and what they sent you, and while you care for a slime together, the shared slime and what you both did for it), go to SettingsPrivacyDelete My Data on the Server. It's removed at once, and caring together stops for both of you.

To remove your slime from the phone, use Start over in Settings, or delete the app.

How do I move my slime to a new phone?

In Settings under Backup, tap Export Save and keep the file in Files, or send it to the new phone. After hatching on the new phone, open Settings and tap Import Save; your old slime replaces the new one. Saves work across iPhone and Android in both directions. Friends aren't part of a save, so add them again after going online.

Does Slimy cost anything?

No. There are no purchases and no ads. Coins are earned in the app, by playing and from daily gifts, and can't be bought. Crates are earned or bought with coins, never with real money.
