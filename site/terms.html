Reports, blocking and bans

  • Report anyone or any chat reply from inside the app. A person reviews every report, normally within 24 hours, and the reported person isn't told who reported them.
  • When content breaks these terms, we act: we reset offending names, remove content and ban the device of the user who posted it. A banned device can no longer use online features and disappears from its friends' lists.
  • Block anyone at any time. Blocking ends the friendship at once, nothing from that device reaches you anymore, and they aren't notified.
  • We may also act without a report when we see a clear breach, and we may delete the server data of devices that abuse the service.

Talk and AI replies

Your slime's replies in Talk are written by AI models from Cloudflare Workers AI and Groq. They are meant to be short and in character, and they can be wrong, odd or unsuitable. They are not advice. Don't rely on them for facts, health, safety, legal, money or any other decision, and don't share personal information in the chat.

Talk is free while free AI capacity lasts. When it runs out, your slime gets sleepy and the chat pauses until later. If a reply bothers you, touch and hold it and tap Report Reply.
