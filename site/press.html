About Slimy

A tiny slime that lives in your Dynamic Island.

Slimy is a pixel-art pet that sits next to your iPhone's camera all day. It wobbles, naps, gets hungry, talks on its own and has real feelings, and it reacts to your battery, charger and Wi-Fi. Touch and hold the Dynamic Island to feed it, play with it, put it to bed, give it a bath or pet it, without opening the app.

Slimy is a pixel-art pet that lives in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. A small emote beside it shows how it feels or what it needs, and touch and hold opens its five needs as buttons that work without opening the app. It reacts to what your iPhone is doing, from a low battery to the first Wi-Fi of the morning.

Inside the app, the slime grows from baby to young to big, with 18 animations at every stage and a personality that drifts over time. It talks on its own and has nine feelings that rise with what happens and settle over time. There are twelve arcade games plus a new game generated every day, eight jelly colours, hats, glasses and scarves, five homes from a cozy room to a crystal cave, crates to open with coins earned in the game, a daily gift calendar, seasonal events and a photo booth. Golden crates hide a secret legendary reward.

Online features are optional. Two people can care for one slime together, each on their own phone. Friends share a code, and their slimes hug, visit and send food, with challenges, a shared garden and a friends leaderboard. A free AI chat lets you talk to your slime, and it answers in the mood it's in. Slimy has no account, no ads, no tracking and nothing to buy. An Android beta brings the same slime to Android 8 and later, where it lives in a notification and in widgets. It's made by hyprnode.

What makes it different

  • It really moves in the island. A Live Activity can't run code. Slimy ships fonts in which every digit is one frame of the slime and lets iOS tick a timer; the seconds digit is the picture.
  • Calm by design. Needs never drop below a resting floor, and the slime never guilt-trips you.
  • Twelve games and one new every day, including Slime Stack and Jelly Dash, plus daily and weekly challenges.
  • A slime with feelings. It talks on its own, and nine feelings change how it moves, what it says and how it answers in chat.
  • Crates with a secret. Wooden, golden and mystery crates, opened with coins earned in the game. Golden crates hide a legendary reward nobody's telling about.
  • One slime, two people. Care Together lets a couple, siblings or roommates look after the same slime on their own phones, iPhone or Android.
  • Friends who visit in the island, a shared weekly garden and a leaderboard for every game.
  • Private by design. Offline first, no account, nothing to buy, no third-party SDKs.
