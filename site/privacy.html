The app without going online

Your slime lives on your iPhone. Its name, colour, needs, feelings, coins, crates, items, homes, records, journal and your chat history are saved only on the device, in Slimy's app container and in the App Group it shares with its widgets. We can't see any of it. Crates are opened on the iPhone; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

To animate your slime, the app reads on the device whether the battery is low or charging, whether Low Power Mode is on, the kind of network connection and the time of day. This information never leaves your iPhone.

A save file you export with Export Save stays wherever you put it. We never receive it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes Slimy's data like any other app's; we have no access to it.

Permissions on your iPhone

Slimy asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and works without any of them.

  • Notifications. Your slime's notifications are scheduled on the iPhone itself. Slimy uses no push service, so we never receive a push token.
  • Photos, add only. When you save a picture from the Photo Booth, Slimy asks to add it to your photo library. It can't see, read or upload any of your photos.
  • Widgets, Live Activities and the Dynamic Island. They show your slime from data on the iPhone. Nothing is sent for them.
  • Background App Refresh. About every 30 minutes, when iOS allows it, Slimy updates your slime on the iPhone. If you're online, it also checks for news from friends and your care partner, with the same data as when the app is open.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Slimy's shortcuts run on the iPhone. If you use them by voice, Apple processes what you say under its own privacy policy.

Slimy never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data or permission to track you.

Caring for a slime together

With Care Together, two people look after one slime, each on their own iPhone. It starts when one of you shares a care code and the other enters it. While you share, our server keeps:

  • The pair: the two device IDs and which of you shared the code.
  • A copy of the shared slime, as the app sends it: its name, colour, level and experience, needs, bond, personality, outfit, feelings, whether it is asleep, its favourite food and game, when it hatched and when it last changed, plus a version number so both iPhones stay in step.
  • Care actions: who fed it, played with it, gave it a bath, petted it, put it to bed, woke it up or gave it a gift, with the food, game or amount involved and the time. They are kept for 30 days.

Only the two of you can read this. Your partner sees your owner name next to what you did, like “Ana fed Blob · 5 min ago”. Your coins, items, records, challenges and settings are not shared and stay on your iPhone. If you join someone else's slime, your own slime waits on your iPhone and isn't sent to us while you share.

When either of you taps Stop Sharing, deletes their server data, blocks the other, or a device is banned, caring together ends for both of you, and the copy of the slime and every care action are deleted at once.

Talk: the AI chat

Talk is free and optional, and works only while you're online. Before your first message, the app asks for your consent and names the services involved; nothing is sent until you tap Agree. With your consent, our server forwards each message to an AI service to write your slime's reply:

  • Cloudflare Workers AI (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) first,
  • Groq (Groq, Inc., USA) when Cloudflare is busy or its free quota for the day is used up.

What is sent: your message, the recent chat (up to 12 turns) and a few facts about your slime: its name, age, level, mood, needs, favourite food, your local time, and how it feels right now (the app sends its current feeling levels, and our server turns them into a few plain words such as “very happy, a little sleepy”). (Slimy 1.0 also sent the name of one friend's slime.) Your owner name and your device ID are never sent to them. The request comes from our server, so they don't see your IP address either.

The AI service answers in your slime's mood and names the feeling its reply expresses. That feeling goes back to your iPhone, where it changes how your slime feels. Our server doesn't keep it.

Our server doesn't store or log any message, reply or feeling. It only counts, per day and per service, how many requests were made and how many failed, without any content. Messages that contain swear words, email addresses or phone numbers aren't sent to an AI service at all; your slime answers them itself. Replies are checked the same way before they reach you. Please don't share personal details in the chat anyway.

What the AI services do with the text: Cloudflare keeps the input and output of a Workers AI request only as long as it needs to answer it, and doesn't use them to train AI models. Groq doesn't keep the text of requests by default; only while it investigates abuse or a technical fault may it keep requests for up to 30 days. Both may use the text only to write the reply for us, never for their own purposes; see Service providers.

You can withdraw your consent any time by turning off Talk with AI in Settings under Privacy, or under About AI Chat in the chat menu. From then on nothing more is sent. Withdrawing doesn't affect messages sent before.

Reports and moderation

If you report something, we store your device ID, the reason and your optional note; for a person, also their device ID; for a chat reply, the text of that reply. A person reviews every report and can reset a name or ban a device. The reported person isn't told who reported them.

Reports stay after either device deletes its data, but without the device IDs from that moment on.

Automatic filters only refuse a name, or let your slime answer a message itself instead of sending it to an AI service. Resetting a name or banning a device is always decided by a person. We make no decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Article 22 GDPR).

Apple and the App Store

Apple runs the App Store. Downloads, updates and the prompt that asks you to rate Slimy are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't learn who you are from them.

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple gives us crash reports and usage statistics for Slimy. Apple prepares them so they don't identify you, and we can't link them to your slime or device ID. App Store Connect also shows us totals such as downloads.

What we never collect

  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • Your Apple ID, email address, phone number or real name, unless you choose to type it as your owner name.
  • Advertising identifiers or hardware identifiers. There is no tracking and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Battery, charger, network or Low Power Mode information.
  • Payment data. Slimy has no in-app purchases and no ads.
  • Third-party analytics or crash reports. Slimy contains no third-party SDKs. The only usage figure we keep ourselves is when a device was last online, to count active devices.
  • Request logs. Neither our API nor the web server keeps a log of the requests it answers. The API writes only its start-up line and technical error messages to the server's system log, never the content of a request, a name or an IP address.
  • IP addresses on disk. They are held in memory for rate limiting only, for about an hour; see below.

Where data is processed

Our server runs in Germany, on hardware hyprnode operates itself; its daily backups stay on the same server. Connections to slime.hyprnode.de, from the app and from this website, are encrypted and pass through Cloudflare's network: Cloudflare decrypts them, protects them against attacks and passes them to our server through an encrypted tunnel. It handles them in its data centres, usually one near you, which can be outside the European Union.

Chat text goes to Cloudflare Workers AI and, as a fallback, to Groq. Both companies are based in the United States and may process data there. These transfers are protected as follows:

  • Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has decided that it ensures an adequate level of protection (Article 45 GDPR). Its data processing agreement also contains the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR) in case the certification stops applying.
  • Groq: the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR), which are part of its data processing agreement.

For a copy of these safeguards, write to us.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Art. 6(1)(b), providing the online features you ask for when you go online and accept the Terms, including Cloudflare passing your connections on.
  • Art. 6(1)(a), your consent, for Talk. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings; that doesn't affect messages sent before.
  • Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in a safe and working service: rate limits, name filters, reports and moderation, backups, counting active devices, and delivering this website through Cloudflare.

Saving data on your iPhone or in your browser, such as your slime, the access token in the keychain or the website's theme, is strictly necessary for what you ask for, so it needs no consent under § 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG.

You don't have to give us any data. Slimy works fully without going online; you just can't use Friends, Talk and Care Together then.

Your rights and deleting data

To delete everything our server stores about your device, open Slimy and go to Settings › Privacy › Delete My Data on the Server. It takes effect at once: your device, profile, friendships, requests, events, challenges, shared-garden points, memories, records and care codes are removed. If you care for a slime together, that ends for both of you and the shared copy and care actions are deleted. Your slime on the iPhone stays.

Deleting the app doesn't delete your data on our server. Use Delete My Data on the Server before you delete the app. If you no longer have the iPhone, write to us with your owner name, your slime's name and roughly when you went online; if that matches exactly one device, we delete it.

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy (Art. 15),
  • have it corrected (Art. 16); you can change your owner name and your slime in the app yourself,
  • have it deleted (Art. 17),
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18),
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable format (Art. 20),
  • withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)),
  • complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us.

Your right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we rely on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

To use any of these rights, write to us; see Changes and contact. We may ask for details to make sure the data is yours.

If you live in the United States: we don't sell your personal information, don't share it for targeted advertising and don't use it for profiling. You can ask what we hold about you, and have it deleted, in the same ways.

Children

Slimy is meant for people aged 13 and over, and you must be at least 13 to go online. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has gone online, contact us and we'll delete the device.
