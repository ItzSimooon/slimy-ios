The app without going online

Your slime lives on your iPhone. Its name, colour, needs, feelings, coins, crates, items, homes, records, journal and your chat history are saved only on the device, in Slimy's app container and in the App Group it shares with its widgets. We can't see any of it. Crates are opened on the iPhone; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

To animate your slime, the app reads on the device whether the battery is low or charging, whether Low Power Mode is on, the kind of network connection and the time of day. This information never leaves your iPhone.

A save file you export with Export Save stays wherever you put it. We never receive it.

Caring for a slime together

With Care Together, two people look after one slime, each on their own iPhone. It starts when one of you shares a care code and the other enters it. While you share, our server keeps:

  • The pair: the two device IDs and which of you shared the code.
  • A copy of the shared slime, as the app sends it: its name, colour, level and experience, needs, bond, personality, outfit, feelings, whether it is asleep, its favourite food and game, when it hatched and when it last changed, plus a version number so both iPhones stay in step.
  • Care actions: who fed it, played with it, gave it a bath, petted it, put it to bed, woke it up or gave it a gift, with the food, game or amount involved and the time. They are kept for 30 days.

Only the two of you can read this. Your partner sees your owner name next to what you did, like “Ana fed Blob · 5 min ago”. Your coins, items, records, challenges and settings are not shared and stay on your iPhone. If you join someone else's slime, your own slime waits on your iPhone and isn't sent to us while you share.

When either of you taps Stop Sharing, deletes their server data, blocks the other, or a device is banned, caring together ends for both of you, and the copy of the slime and every care action are deleted at once.

Talk: the AI chat

Talk is free and optional. Before your first message, the app asks for your consent and names the services involved. With your consent, our server forwards each message to an AI service to write your slime's reply:

  • Cloudflare Workers AI (Cloudflare, Inc.) first,
  • Groq (Groq, Inc.) when Cloudflare is busy or its free quota for the day is used up.

What is sent: your message, the recent chat (up to 12 turns) and a few facts about your slime: its name, age, level, mood, needs, favourite food, your local time, and how it feels right now (the app sends its current feeling levels, and our server turns them into a few plain words such as “very happy, a little sleepy”). (Slimy 1.0 also sent the name of one friend's slime.) Your owner name and your device ID are never sent to them.

The AI service answers in your slime's mood and names the feeling its reply expresses. That feeling goes back to your iPhone, where it changes how your slime feels. Our server doesn't keep it.

Our server doesn't store or log any message, reply or feeling. It only counts, per day and per service, how many requests were made and how many failed, without any content. Messages that contain swear words, email addresses or phone numbers aren't sent to an AI service at all; your slime answers them itself. Replies are checked the same way before they reach you.

The AI services process the text to produce the reply, under their own terms and privacy policies: Cloudflare and Groq. You can withdraw your consent any time by turning off Talk with AI in Settings under Privacy.

Reports and moderation

If you report someone, we store your device ID, the reported device ID, the reason, your optional note and, for a reported chat reply, the text of that reply. A person reviews every report and can reset a name or ban a device. The reported person isn't told who reported them.

Reports stay after either device deletes its data, but without the device IDs from that moment on.

What we never collect

  • Your location, contacts, photos or health data.
  • Your Apple ID, email address, phone number or real name, unless you choose to type it as your owner name.
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifiers. There is no tracking and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Battery, charger, network or Low Power Mode information.
  • Third-party analytics or crash reports. Slimy contains no third-party SDKs. The only usage figure we keep is when a device was last online, to the nearest 10 minutes, to count active devices.
  • Request logs. Neither our API nor the web server keeps a log of the requests it answers.
  • IP addresses on disk. They are held in memory for rate limiting only, for about an hour; see below.

Where data is processed

Our server runs in Germany, operated by hyprnode. Connections to slime.hyprnode.de, from the app and from this website, are encrypted and pass through Cloudflare's network, which delivers them to our server. Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP addresses for that purpose and to protect against attacks.

Chat text goes to Cloudflare Workers AI and, as a fallback, to Groq. Both companies are based in the United States and may process the text outside the European Union.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. Every font, image and script comes from this server; nothing is loaded from other sites.
  • The web server keeps no access log. Cloudflare delivers the site as described above.
  • If you pick a theme or a slime colour on this site, your browser remembers it in its local storage. It never leaves your browser, and clearing site data removes it.
  • An invite page such as /f/K7Q2MD asks our server who made that code: the owner name, the slime's name, colour and stage, and when the code expires. Looking doesn't use the code up. Lookups are rate-limited per IP address, in memory.
  • The download buttons read the newest version from a file on this server, so your browser doesn't contact GitHub until you click a link to it.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Art. 6(1)(b), providing the online features you ask for when you go online and accept the Terms.
  • Art. 6(1)(a), your consent, for Talk. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
  • Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in a safe and working service: rate limits, name filters, reports and moderation, backups and counting active devices.

Your rights and deleting data

To delete everything our server stores about your device, open Slimy and go to Settings › Privacy › Delete My Data on the Server. It takes effect at once: your device, profile, friendships, requests, events, challenges, shared-garden points, memories, records and care codes are removed. If you care for a slime together, that ends for both of you and the shared copy and care actions are deleted. Your slime on the iPhone stays.

You also have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

Children

Slimy is meant for people aged 13 and over. You must be at least 13 to go online. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you use Talk. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has gone online, contact us and we'll delete the device.
