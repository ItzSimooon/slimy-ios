          How does a Live Activity move?

          It can't run code. But iOS keeps a timer ticking every second on its own. Slimy ships fonts in which every digit is one frame of the slime, and the island shows only the last digit. Six timers stacked, one per colour, make the coloured slime.

            Privacy

            Private by design.

            Your slime lives on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, nothing to buy.

            Offline first. Until you go online for Friends, Talk or caring together, Slimy sends nothing anywhere.

            No account. Going online gives your phone a random ID. No email, no phone number, no sign-in.

            Your phone stays yours. Battery, charger and network are read on the phone to animate the slime and never sent.

            Chat isn't kept. Messages and how your slime feels pass to the AI to write a reply. No name or device ID goes with them, and nothing is stored.

            Delete any time. Delete My Data on the Server, in Settings, removes your online profile at once. Your slime stays on the phone.

            No trackers here either. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers.

            Questions

            Good to know.

            Is Slimy free?

            Yes. There are no purchases and no ads. Coins and crates are earned in the app and can't be bought with real money, and the AI chat is free too.

            Can two people look after one slime?

            Yes. Share your slime with one person and you both care for it on your own phones, iPhone or Android: feeding, playing, a bath, a pet or bedtime on one phone shows up on the other. Coins, items and records stay your own. More in Support.

            Why isn't it on the App Store yet?

            Slimy: Island Pet is being prepared for App Store review. Until it's there, you can install the beta from GitHub with Sideloadly. With a free Apple ID you re-sign it every 7 days.

            My slime left the Dynamic Island. Is it gone?

            No. iOS ends every Live Activity after about 8 hours. Open Slimy, or use Summon Slime from Control Center or the Action Button, and it's back.

            Which phones does it work on?

            Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The island needs an iPhone that has a Dynamic Island: iPhone 14 Pro and most models since. Other iPhones get the slime on the Lock Screen and in widgets.

            Android phones with Android 8 or later can install the Android beta. There your slime gets its own island around the front camera, plus a notification and widgets.

            Does it need the internet?

            No. The slime, its games, its crates and its home work offline. Only Friends, Talk and caring together go online, and only after you say so.
