Sideloading uses Apple's free developer signing. It's meant for apps you build or test yourself, which is what this beta is. Nothing here jailbreaks or changes your iPhone.

You need

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or laterThe island needs an iPhone that has a Dynamic Island: iPhone 14 Pro and most models since. Other iPhones get the Lock Screen and widgets.
  • A Windows PC or a MacWith Sideloadly. On Windows, also iTunes and iCloud from apple.com (not the Microsoft Store versions), which bring the drivers Sideloadly needs.
  • An Apple IDA free one works. Apple uses it to sign the app for your iPhone.
  • A USB cableTo connect the iPhone to the computer for the install and for re-signing.

Install it with Sideloadly

  1. Get Sideloadly ready

    Install Sideloadly on your computer. On Windows, install iTunes and iCloud from apple.com first, then restart the computer.

  2. Connect your iPhone

    Plug it in with a cable and unlock it. When the iPhone asks whether to trust this computer, tap Trust and enter your passcode.

  3. Install Slimy

    Open Sideloadly, choose your iPhone, drag Slimy.ipa onto the window, enter your Apple ID and click Start. Enter the code Apple sends if you use two-factor authentication.

    Sideloadly asks Apple for a signing certificate with your Apple ID. If you'd rather keep your main account out of it, a second Apple ID works just as well.

  4. Trust the app on your iPhone

    Open SettingsGeneralVPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID under Developer App and tap Trust.

    If iOS asks for Developer Mode: SettingsPrivacy & SecurityDeveloper Mode, turn it on, restart, and confirm.

  5. Hatch your slime

    Open Slimy, tap the egg, give your slime a name and a colour, and tap Hatch. Then tap Bring It to the Island, and your slime moves in next to the camera.

Every 7 days: re-sign

With a free Apple ID, Apple's signature lasts 7 days. After that, Slimy won't open until it's signed again. Your slime is safe in the meantime.

  • Connect your iPhone, open Sideloadly and install the newest Slimy.ipa again with the same Apple ID. Your slime stays, because the app is replaced, not deleted.
  • Don't delete the app before re-signing. Deleting it deletes the slime.
  • Updating works the same way: install a new Slimy.ipa over the old one.
  • To be safe, export a save now and then: SettingsBackupExport Save in Slimy. Import it on any iPhone.
  • A free Apple ID can keep three sideloaded apps on a device at a time. A paid Apple Developer account signs for a year.

Keep it in the island all day

About 8 hours at a time

iOS ends every Live Activity after about 8 hours. Open Slimy and your slime comes right back; it also renews an activity that's close to the limit.

Summon Slime

Add Summon Slime to Control Center or the Action Button to bring your slime back without opening the app. Siri and Shortcuts know it too.

Settings that help

Leave Live Activities and Background App Refresh on for Slimy in the Settings app, and keep Always bring it back on in Slimy's Settings under Dynamic Island.

If something doesn't work

Sideloadly can't find my iPhone.

Unlock the iPhone and tap Trust when it asks. On Windows, make sure iTunes and iCloud are the apple.com versions, not the Microsoft Store ones, then restart Sideloadly. Try another cable or port.

"Untrusted Developer" when I open Slimy.

Trust your Apple ID once: SettingsGeneralVPN & Device Management, tap it and tap Trust.

Sideloadly says I've reached the app limit.

A free Apple ID can have three sideloaded apps installed at a time. Remove one you don't need, or use another Apple ID.

The widgets stay empty.

Open Slimy once so the widgets get your slime, then give them a moment. If you installed Slimy-unsigned.ipa, or your tool removed the App Group, widgets can't see your slime. Install Slimy.ipa instead.
