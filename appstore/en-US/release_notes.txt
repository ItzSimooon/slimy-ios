Slimy 1.1 brings crates, caring together and a slime with feelings.

• Crates: buy wooden and golden crates with coins, earn mystery crates, and tap them open for coins, food, outfits, furniture and new colours. Golden crates hide a secret legendary. Nobody's telling.
• The app shows the drop rates for every crate, and duplicates turn into coins
• Care Together: share your slime with one person and look after it together, each on your own iPhone
• Your slime has real feelings now. They change how it moves, what it says and how it answers in chat
• It talks on its own while the app is open. Prefer a quieter slime? Turn off Talks on its own in Settings
• The chat answers in your slime's mood, and what you say can change it
• Friends are all about hugs, visits, gifts, greetings, challenges and the shared garden

Thank you for looking after your slime.
