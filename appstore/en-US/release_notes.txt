Slimy 1.0 is here.

• A pixel slime that lives in your Dynamic Island, on the Lock Screen and in widgets
• 12 arcade games, including the new Slime Stack and Jelly Dash, plus a new game every day
• Daily streaks and a small gift every day
• Photo booth: pose your slime and share the picture
• Friends leaderboard for every game
• Invite friends with a link
• Talk to your slime, with a clear choice before anything is sent
• Light and dark mode
• Back up your slime to a file and restore it
