A tiny pixel slime that lives in your Dynamic Island.

It sits next to the camera all day. It wobbles, naps, gets hungry, talks on its own and reacts to what your iPhone is doing. Touch and hold the island to feed it, play with it, give it a bath or pet it, without opening the app.

NEW IN 1.1
• Crates: buy wooden and golden crates with coins, earn mystery crates, and tap them open for coins, food, outfits, furniture and new colours. Golden crates hide a secret legendary. Nobody's telling.
• Care Together: share your slime with one person, like your partner, a sibling or a roommate. You both look after the same slime on your own iPhones and see who fed it last.
• Real feelings: joy, curiosity, boredom, sleepiness and more rise with what happens and change how your slime moves and what it says
• It talks on its own, every few seconds, about its day, the weather, the time or you

ALWAYS WITH YOU
• Lives in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and shows up in StandBy
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show how it feels and what it needs, and the medium widget can feed and pet it
• Notices your iPhone: low battery makes it melt, the charger gives it a little zap, and it reacts to Wi-Fi, mobile data, Low Power Mode, mornings and nights
• Controls for Control Center and the Action Button to summon, feed and pet it or put it to bed, plus Siri and Shortcuts
• No Dynamic Island? Your slime still lives on the Lock Screen and in widgets

A SLIME OF ITS OWN
• Five needs: food, fun, energy, clean and love. They never drop to zero, even if you are away for weeks, and your slime never guilt-trips you
• Its own personality, playful, cuddly, foodie, sleepy or curious, that changes over time
• Find out its secret favourite food and favourite game
• It grows from baby to young to big, with its own animations at every stage and a journal of memories
• Set a bedtime and it goes to sleep with you
• Keep a daily streak and open a small gift every day

12 ARCADE GAMES AND A NEW ONE EVERY DAY
Snack Catcher, Slime Jump, Bubble Pop, Memory Garden, Slime Racing, Rhythm Bounce, Garden Puzzle, Treasure Dig, Slime Basketball, Color Match, Slime Stack and Jelly Dash. More games unlock as your slime levels up. Every game has a short tutorial and keeps your records. Today's game is different every day and comes with a small quest.

DRESS UP AND DECORATE
Hats, glasses, scarves, effects and furniture. Five homes to move into: a room, a garden, a beach, space and a crystal cave. Strike a pose in the photo booth and share the picture.

Coins are earned in the app, from games, daily gifts and achievements, and they buy crates too. The app shows the chances for every crate. There is nothing to buy with real money.

FRIENDS, IF YOU WANT
Send a code or a link and your slimes become friends. Hug, visit, send food and greetings, play the same board in a challenge, grow a shared garden every week and compare records on the friends leaderboard. Now and then a friend's slime drops by in your Dynamic Island.

TALK TO YOUR SLIME
Ask it something and it answers in a few words, in character and in the mood it's in. A kind word can cheer it up. Replies are written by an AI service. Slimy asks before your first message and tells you exactly what is sent. You can report any reply.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your slime lives on your iPhone and works offline
• Friends, caring together and talking connect only after you choose to go online
• Delete your server data in the app at any time
• Back up your slime to a file and restore it

Slimy needs iOS 18. The app is in English.
